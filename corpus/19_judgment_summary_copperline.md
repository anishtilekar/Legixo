# Judgment summary — CV-2025-1190 (fiction)

**Matter:** Copperline Studios LLP v. Vantage Freight Services Pvt. Ltd.
**Date of judgment:** 12 September 2025

## Finding

The court held that extra declared-value cover **was** purchased, because the waybill
carried a surcharge line item that Vantage could not explain. The limitation-of-liability
cap therefore did not apply.

## Relief granted

- Damages: **₹8,50,000**
- Interest: **9% per year** from the date of demand until payment
- Costs: **₹65,000**

The claim for loss of business reputation was **dismissed** as too remote.
