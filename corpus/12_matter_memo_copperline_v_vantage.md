# Internal memo — Civil Suit No. CV-2025-1190 (fiction)

**Matter:** Copperline Studios LLP v. Vantage Freight Services Pvt. Ltd.
**Court:** District Court, Riverside Bench (fictional)
**Stage:** Final arguments

## Summary

Copperline claims that camera equipment shipped in March 2025 arrived damaged and that
Vantage refused to honour the declared-value cover.

## Key dates

- Consignment booked: 6 March 2025.
- Damage reported on delivery: 11 March 2025.
- Declared value on the waybill: **₹9,40,000**.

## Position

Vantage relies on a limitation-of-liability clause capping claims at **₹50,000** unless
extra cover was purchased. Copperline says extra cover was in fact purchased and paid for.
