# Lease excerpt — Warehouse Unit W-2, Dockside Estate (fiction)

**Lessor:** Dockside Warehousing Co.
**Lessee:** Northfield Logistics Pvt. Ltd.

## Rent and deposit

Monthly rent: **₹1,20,000**. Security deposit held: **₹3,60,000**. Deposit refundable
within **30 days** of handover if no damage beyond normal wear.

## Permitted use

The unit may be used only for **storage and cross-docking of non-hazardous goods**. Storage
of flammable material is prohibited without written approval.

## Termination

Either party may terminate on **90 days** written notice after the first year.
