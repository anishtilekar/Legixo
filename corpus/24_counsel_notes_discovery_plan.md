**Matter:** Arvind Mehta v. Northfield Logistics (CV-2024-8812)

## Documents to request

- Northfield's proof-of-delivery scans for all March–June 2024 consignments.
- The damage inspection report referenced in the defence but not annexed.

production must be raised within **10 days** of service, failing which they are waived.
