# Order sheet extract — CV-2025-0733 (fiction)

**Matter:** Meridian Health Analytics Ltd. v. Bluecrest Analytics LLP
**Date of order:** 18 July 2025

## Order

The defendant is restrained from further processing the licensed dataset pending disposal
of the interim application.

The injunction operates **only** on records created after 31 December 2024; archived
records may be retained but not processed.

As a condition of the injunction, the plaintiff shall deposit **₹5,00,000** with the
registry as security for costs within **fourteen days**.

## Next date

Compliance to be reported on **5 November 2025**.
