# Internal memo — Civil Suit No. CV-2023-4401 (fiction)

**Matter:** Lakeview Society v. City Water Board
**Court:** District Court, Riverside Bench (fictional)
**Stage:** Interim relief

## Summary

The society claims the water board reduced supply to the colony without notice. The board
says the reduction was a scheduled maintenance measure notified by public circular.

## Key dates

- Supply reduction began: 4 February 2025.
- Interim application filed: 19 February 2025.
- Hearing adjourned to: **22 September 2025**.

## Position

Counsel assesses the balance of convenience favours the society because the reduction
affects **340 households**. Interim relief was not granted at the first hearing.
