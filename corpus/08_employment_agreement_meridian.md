# Employment agreement excerpt — Meridian Health Analytics (fiction)

**Employee:** Sunita Rao
**Employer:** Meridian Health Analytics Ltd.

## Notice period

Either party may end this agreement by giving **90 days** written notice. Meridian may place
the employee on **garden leave** for all or part of the notice period, during which salary
continues but access to systems is withdrawn.

## Non-solicitation

For **18 months** after leaving, the employee may not solicit any Meridian employee above
grade M3 to join a competing business.

## Confidentiality

Patient-derived datasets remain confidential **without time limit**, even after the
employment ends.
