# Client engagement letter excerpt (fiction)

**Client:** Copperline Studios LLP
**Matter:** Recovery of damages against Vantage Freight (CV-2025-1190)

## Fees

Hourly rate for the responsible partner: **₹12,000**. Associate rate: **₹5,500**. An initial
retainer of **₹1,50,000** is payable before work begins and is adjusted against the first
invoice.

## Billing cycle

Invoices are raised **monthly** and payable within **fifteen days** of receipt.

## Termination

The client may terminate the engagement at any time in writing; fees for work already done
remain payable. The firm may withdraw only for good cause and with reasonable notice.
