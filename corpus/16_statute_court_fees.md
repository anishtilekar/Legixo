# Fictional statute excerpt — "Riverside Court Fees Schedule (sample text only)"

**Item 1 — Ad valorem fee on money claims**

Where the claim does not exceed ten lakh fictional rupees, the fee is **2% of the claim
value**. Where the claim exceeds ten lakh, the fee is **1.5%** of the amount above ten lakh
plus the fee on the first ten lakh.

**Item 4 — Maximum fee**

The total court fee payable on any single suit is capped at **₹2,00,000**.

**Item 9 — Refund on settlement**

Where a suit settles before evidence begins, **half** the court fee paid is refundable on
application.
