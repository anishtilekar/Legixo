# Witness statement summary (fiction)

**Matter:** Arvind Mehta v. Northfield Logistics (CV-2024-8812)
**Witness:** Billing head, plaintiff company

## Summary of evidence

The witness will state that invoices were raised on completion of each consignment and that
proof-of-delivery was collected in every case except one.

The witness will confirm that no written complaint about damage was received before
**18 June 2024**, the date the final invoice was disputed.

## Documents to be produced

The consignment register and the invoice ledger for March to June 2024.

The witness is available on the next listed date and does not require an interpreter.
