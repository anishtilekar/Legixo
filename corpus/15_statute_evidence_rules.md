# Fictional statute excerpt — "Riverside Evidence Rules (sample text only)"

**Rule 31 — Expert affidavits**

An expert affidavit must be filed at least **fourteen days** before the hearing at which the
expert is to be examined. The opposing party may file a reply affidavit within seven days
of service.

**Rule 44 — Electronic records**

An electronic record is admissible only if accompanied by a **certificate of authenticity**
signed by the person in lawful control of the device that produced it.

**Rule 52 — Hostile witnesses**

Leave of the court is required before cross-examining a party's own witness. Leave is
recorded in the order sheet with reasons.
