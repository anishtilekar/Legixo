# IP assignment excerpt — Copperline Studios (fiction)

**Assignor:** Freelance contributor
**Assignee:** Copperline Studios LLP

## Assignment

All work product created under a commissioned brief vests in Copperline on creation. Where
vesting on creation is not effective in law, the contributor assigns it **on payment of the
final invoice**.

## Moral rights

The contributor waives moral rights to the extent permitted, but retains the right to be
**credited by name** in the end titles of any released production.

## Pre-existing material

Pre-existing material remains the contributor's property; Copperline receives a
**perpetual, royalty-free licence** to use it only within the commissioned work.
