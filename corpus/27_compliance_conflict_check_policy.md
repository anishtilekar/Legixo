# Compliance policy — conflict checks (fiction)

## When a check is required

A conflict check must be completed **before** any engagement letter is issued, and repeated
whenever a new party joins an existing matter.

## Search scope

The check searches current and former clients, opposing parties, and related entities for
the previous **six years**.

## Clearing a conflict

A conflict may be cleared only with **informed written consent** from every affected client.
Consent recorded by telephone is not sufficient.

## Escalation

Unresolved conflicts are escalated to the responsible partner within **two working days**.
