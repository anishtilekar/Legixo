# Vendor SLA excerpt — Northfield / Dockside (fiction)

**Customer:** Northfield Logistics Pvt. Ltd.
**Vendor:** Dockside Warehousing Co.

## Service levels

The vendor shall maintain warehouse management system uptime of **99.5%** measured monthly,
excluding scheduled maintenance notified at least 48 hours in advance.

## Service credits

- Uptime below 99.5% but at or above 98%: credit of **5%** of the monthly fee.
- Uptime below 98%: credit of **15%** of the monthly fee.

Service credits are the customer's sole financial remedy for downtime.

## Termination for repeated failure

Three consecutive months below 98% entitle the customer to terminate on **60 days** notice.
