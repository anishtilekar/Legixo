# Counsel notes — strategy, CV-2025-0733 (fiction)

**Matter:** Meridian Health Analytics v. Bluecrest Analytics

The oral-renewal defence is the weak point. Bluecrest has produced an invoice but no
counter-signed renewal, and the licence requires amendments to be **in writing and signed**.

Client instruction: press for summary disposal of the oral-renewal defence rather than a
full trial on damages.

Do not concede the archived-records point — the injunction already permits retention, so
conceding gains nothing.
