# Compliance policy — data retention (fiction)

**Applies to:** all matter files and business records

## Retention periods

- Financial and billing records: **seven years** from the end of the financial year.
- Personnel and HR records: **three years** after the employee leaves.
- Matter files for closed litigation: **ten years** from final disposal.
- CCTV footage: **30 days**, unless preserved for an active matter.

## Legal hold

Where litigation is reasonably anticipated, the retention clock **stops** and destruction is
suspended until the hold is lifted in writing by the responsible partner.

## Destruction

Destruction must be logged with the date, the record class, and the approving partner.
