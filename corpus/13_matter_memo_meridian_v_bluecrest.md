# Internal memo — Civil Suit No. CV-2025-0733 (fiction)

**Matter:** Meridian Health Analytics Ltd. v. Bluecrest Analytics LLP
**Court:** Commercial Division, Riverside Bench (fictional)
**Stage:** Interim application

## Summary

Meridian alleges Bluecrest continued to use a licensed dataset after the licence expired on
**31 December 2024**. Bluecrest says a renewal was agreed orally and invoiced.

## Key dates

- Licence commencement: 1 January 2023.
- Licence expiry: 31 December 2024.
- Alleged unauthorised use detected: 14 March 2025.

## Position

Because the claim value exceeds five lakh, the matter was referred to **mandatory
mediation** before trial. Mediation concluded without settlement on 2 June 2025.
