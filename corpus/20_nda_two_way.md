# Two-way NDA excerpt — Bluecrest / Meridian (fiction)

**Parties:** Bluecrest Analytics LLP and Meridian Health Analytics Ltd.
**Effective date:** 1 January 2023

## Term

Confidentiality obligations continue for **three years** from the effective date, except for
trade secrets, which remain protected for as long as they qualify as trade secrets.

## Carve-outs

Information is **not** confidential if it: was already public; was lawfully received from a
third party without restriction; or was independently developed without reference to the
disclosure.

## Return of materials

On written request, each party must return or destroy confidential materials within
**thirty days** and certify destruction in writing.
