# Costs schedule — standard scale (fiction)

## Party-and-party costs

Costs follow the event unless the court orders otherwise. The successful party recovers
costs on the **standard scale**, not on an indemnity basis, unless misconduct is found.

## Scale

- Suits up to ₹5,00,000: costs capped at **₹40,000**.
- Suits above ₹5,00,000 and up to ₹25,00,000: costs capped at **₹65,000**.
- Suits above ₹25,00,000: assessed by the taxing officer.

## Interim applications

Costs of an interim application are ordinarily **costs in the cause**, meaning they follow
the final outcome of the suit rather than being paid immediately.
