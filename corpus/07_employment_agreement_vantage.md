# Employment agreement excerpt — Vantage Freight Services (fiction)

**Employee:** Rohit Desai
**Employer:** Vantage Freight Services Pvt. Ltd.

## Notice period

Either party may end this agreement by giving **30 days** written notice. The employer may
pay salary in lieu of notice at its sole discretion.

## Probation

The first **three months** are probationary. During probation, either side may terminate on
**seven days** notice without assigning a reason.

## Non-compete

For **6 months** after leaving, the employee may not solicit any customer that the employee
personally serviced in the final year of employment.
